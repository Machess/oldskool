The small print, in plain words

Privacy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

We make games for children, so we collect as little personal information as we can. This page explains what we do collect, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

Who we are

Oldschool is run by [LEGAL NAME OF THE COMPANY OR OWNER], based in [COUNTRY]. We are the "controller" of the personal information described on this page. You can reach us at any time through the contact form.

This website

  • No tracking. This website sets no cookies, shows no ads and uses no analytics or tracking tools.
  • No outside content. The fonts, styles and scripts are served from this website itself, so your browser is not sent to other companies when you read these pages.
  • Server logs. Like every website, ours is stored with a hosting company, [HOSTING PROVIDER]. Its servers may record technical details such as your IP address, browser type and the time of your visit, to keep the site running and secure.

When you write to us

If you use the contact form, we receive the name, email address and message you type in. We use them only to read and answer your message. We do not add you to a mailing list, and we never sell or share your details for marketing.

The form is delivered to our inbox by a form service, [FORM SERVICE], which handles the message on our behalf. Our email is provided by Microsoft Outlook. We keep messages only as long as we need them to deal with your request, and then delete them.

The contact form is meant for adults. Children, please ask a parent or guardian to write for you.

Our games

  • No ads, ever. Our games contain no advertising and no advertising trackers.
  • No in-game purchases, ever. There is nothing to buy inside a game, so no payment details are collected in play.

Why we are allowed to use this information

We answer contact messages because you asked us to and because it is in our legitimate interest to reply to people who write to us. Hosting logs are kept because it is in our legitimate interest to run a safe, working website.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct it if it is wrong;
  • delete it;
  • stop or limit how we use it.

Parents and guardians can ask for the same on behalf of their child. To do any of this, write to us through the contact form. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you also have the right to complain to the data protection authority in your country.

Changes to this page

If we change how we handle personal information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
